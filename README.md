# JvsNether Structures

The Nether has never felt so alive.

**JvsNether Structures** fills the Nether with hand-crafted structures that breathe life into every biome. Whether you're crossing the desolate Nether Wastes, hunting through the crimson undergrowth, wandering the eerie warped forest, or navigating the ashen Soul Sand Valley... something new is waiting to be discovered.

## What to Expect

Each biome tells its own story through its structures:

**Nether Wastes** : The heart of piglin territory. Ancient ruins, towering bone formations, mysterious eye towers, and relics of a forgotten civilization scatter the landscape. Piglins have marked this land as their own.

**Crimson Forest** : A hostile and untamed wilderness. Orclin camps hide between the fungal growth, and the remnants of piglin activity echo through cauldrons and shipyards built along the forest's edge.

**Warped Forest** : Strange and unsettling. Piglin camps push into this alien biome, and the remains of abyss shipyards suggest someone... or something... was once trying to leave.

**Soul Sand Valley** : A graveyard of the Nether. Bone structures emerge from the soul sand, half-buried and half-forgotten.

**Across the Nether's Seas** : Piglin ships drift on the lava, remnants of fleets that once sailed the fiery oceans. Some are intact. Some are not.
